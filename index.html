Our story

Born from bedtime stories and a grandmother's lap

WunderCubs began with a simple question: why do our children's toys have nothing to do with the world they come from?

My daughter reached for her grandmother's phone instead of the plush toy sitting right next to it. That was the moment I knew something had to change.— Sravani, Founder

How it started

WunderCubs was founded in 2026 by a parent who noticed her toddler gravitating toward screens over stories. The solution wasn't to ban technology—it was to make the alternative irresistible.

Working with artisans, child-development experts, and cultural storytellers, we imagine plush toys that do something screens cannot: they invite a child to imagine, to feel, and to connect with something real.

Our mission

We believe the earliest years of a child's life are sacred. The stories they hear, the textures they touch, and the characters they love shape who they become.

WunderCubs exists to fill those years with toys rooted in India's living traditions—Ganesha's wisdom, Hanuman's courage, the warmth of a dadi's voice—brought to life in soft, screen-free companions that little hands can hold.
