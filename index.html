Our story

Born from bedtime stories and a grandmother's lap

WunderCubs began with a simple question: why do our children's toys have nothing to do with the world they come from?

"

My daughter reached for her grandmother's phone instead of the plush toy sitting right next to it. That was the moment I knew something had to change.

- Sravani, Founder
1

The moment

WunderCubs was founded in 2026 by a parent who noticed her toddler gravitating toward screens over stories - and decided the alternative needed to be irresistible, not just "allowed."

2

The mission

We set out to fill a gap on the shelf: toys that carry India's living traditions, so children form their sense of identity through play, not just entertainment.

3

The making

Working with child-development experts and cultural storytellers, we design plush companions that invite a child to imagine, feel, and connect with something real.

Our mission

Filling childhood with stories worth holding onto

We believe the earliest years of a child's life are sacred. The stories they hear, the textures they touch, and the characters they love shape who they become.

WunderCubs exists to fill those years with toys rooted in India's living traditions - Ganesha's wisdom, Hanuman's courage, the warmth of a dadi's voice - brought to life in soft, screen-free companions that little hands can hold.
